How a night works

Three steps from "anyone up for a game?" to turn one.

  1. 1

    Bring a deck

    Paste any list, drop in a Moxfield or Archidekt link, or grab one of the hundred most-played decks on Archidekt and make it yours. Pick the printing and foil for every card. Up to twenty decks live on your account; your default is seated for you automatically.

  2. 2

    Open a table

    Host a table and share the five-letter code in Discord, or list it publicly and let anyone pull up a chair. Two, three or four seats; fill the empty ones with bots so the game never waits on a fourth.

  3. 3

    Play

    Each player keeps a private hand and library. Tap, attack, block, scry, surveil, add counters, track commander tax and life, talk in table chat. Friends who aren't playing can watch the whole thing live.

What's at the table

Everything a pod needs, nothing it has to install.

Bots that read the cards

Practice bots don't follow a script. They read each card's oracle text and act on it: triggers on entering and dying, landfall, counters, proliferate, scry and surveil, equipment, sagas, planeswalkers, cascade, tokens, removal, fights.

Mana done properly

Bots pay the real cost: colored pips, hybrid, Phyrexian, colorless. A Wastes never pays for a Llanowar Elves. Command Tower knows the commander's colors, Signets net one, Treasures crack when needed.

Board-wide rules respected

Play Rule of Law and the bots cast one spell a turn. Rhystic Study, Propaganda, Kismet, Torpor Orb, Winter Orb, Blind Obedience: pay-or-else taxes, attack costs and enters-tapped effects apply in both directions.

Live tables, live spectators

Real-time rooms keep every seat in sync. Spectators join with the code and see the board and the log while hands stay hidden. Table chat lasts for the game and clears when it ends.

Commander tools built in

Automatic commander tax with manual adjustments for partner decks, life totals and poison, +1/+1 and loyalty counters, scry and surveil dialogs, a searchable library, graveyard and exile, and a full action log.

Decks worth looking at

See every card's art while you edit, right-click to swap in any printing or foil, favorite a deck, export the list. Import straight from Moxfield or Archidekt links, or start from the Top 100.

Phone, tablet, desktop

Install it as an app and play full-screen. Cards never get cut off at any size, and a performance mode keeps weaker phones smooth without holding back the big screens.

Clubs and win boards

Make a club for your playgroup. Members, shared game history and a win board, so the rivalry has somewhere to live. Sign in with Discord or email; guests can play too.
