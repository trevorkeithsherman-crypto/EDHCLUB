How a night works

Three steps from "anyone up for a game?" to turn one.

  1. 1

    Bring a deck

    Paste any list, drop in a Moxfield or Archidekt link, or grab one of the hundred most-played decks on Archidekt and make it yours. Pick the printing and foil for every card. Up to twenty decks live on your account; your default is seated for you automatically.

  2. 2

    Open a table

    Host a table and share the five-letter code in Discord, or list it publicly and let anyone pull up a chair. Two, three or four seats; fill the empty ones with bots so the game never waits on a fourth.

  3. 3

    Play

    Each player keeps a private hand and library. Tap, attack, block, scry, surveil, add counters, track commander tax and life, talk in table chat. Friends who aren't playing can watch the whole thing live.

Board-wide rules respected
